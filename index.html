FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can I bring someone with me?

I kindly ask that clients attend alone. If you have a special circumstance, you can direct message @lasesklinique beforehand.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, a patch test is required 2–3 days before your appointment to check for any potential sensitivities or reactions. If you choose to opt out, an online waiver must be signed. Làsesklinique will not be held liable for any reactions.

Can I book an Emergency Appointment?

Emergency and squeeze-in appointments are subject to availability for an additional €15 fee. Please DM @lasesklinique directly to enquire.
